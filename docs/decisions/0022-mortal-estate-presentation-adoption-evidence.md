# Authorize bounded Mortal Estate presentation-adoption evidence; keep R1 closed

## Decision authority

Peter Permenter authorized the paired Nomos and The Mortal Estate adoption
proposal on 2026-08-26. This record authorizes only the bounded evidence program
defined below. It does not adopt Nomos into a game, amend R1, or authorize R2.

The Mortal Estate records its own resumption, target, experiment order, and
final disposition through that repository's existing owners. This Nomos record
cannot confer authority on another project.

## Context

Decision 0019 accepted R1 as this repository's runtime baseline and explicitly
did not adopt Nomos into a game. It closed the implementation epoch and required
a new owner decision before a later runtime epoch, capability family, or game
adoption program.

The Mortal Estate is the concrete game for which the underlying visual-authoring
problem was encountered repeatedly. Its owner reports three or four abandoned
visual pipeline attempts: the work asked an LLM to produce or coordinate output
at a level where it was not reliable. Nomos was then built in a vacuum to test a
different allocation of responsibility: the author names typed intent, semantic
owners resolve facts, projection compilers own consequences, and the renderer
owns final pixels.

The adopter is not greenfield. It already owns gameplay truth, a deterministic
rules boundary, authored content, an authoring compiler, a persistent server,
wire contracts, a Godot client, and a presentation seam. That makes duplicate
authority the primary integration risk and presentation the narrowest honest
first test.

## Decision

Authorize one bounded external-adoption evidence program for The Mortal Estate,
starting with presentation. The program asks whether a pinned Nomos
presentation-authoring and compilation boundary can produce an owner-accepted
Mortal Estate gameplay look while:

- asking content authors for typed intent rather than raw transforms or pixels;
- consuming adopter-owned resolved facts without recomputing gameplay truth;
- keeping reusable Nomos changes adopter-neutral and upstream-first;
- keeping adopter mechanics, content, identity, platform verdict, and taste
  authority outside Nomos; and
- recording enough cost and failure evidence to decide whether any deeper
  adoption or narrow R2 is warranted.

The first renderer candidate is the existing browser/WebGL path because it is
the cheapest relevant evidence already carried by Nomos. This is an experiment
order, not a platform decision. A Godot comparison is permitted only when it
consumes the same admitted artifacts and resolves a named consequential
uncertainty.

## What this authorizes

1. A content-addressed adoption plan and evidence schema under `docs/evaluation/`
   after its own review.
2. Inspection of public, owner-approved Mortal Estate contracts and sanitized
   recorded projections as adopter inputs.
3. A disposable integration lab outside both repositories, bound to exact
   commits, trees, inputs, commands, artifacts, and measurements.
4. Adopter-neutral minimal failing fixtures under `experiments/` when a finding
   has first been classified as a Nomos defect or reusable missing capability.
   Such a fixture remains non-authoritative and satisfies no acceptance.
5. Separately falsifiable issues for an immutable dependency point, target
   evidence, integration evidence, and final owner disposition.

## What this does not authorize

- no amendment to R1 or `RUNTIME.md`;
- no Nomos implementation change, new accepted capability family, or R2 work;
- no Gate K retry, protocol revision, or reinterpretation of old evidence;
- no claim that The Signed World applies to The Mortal Estate;
- no import of Mortal Estate content, identities, routes, coordinates, palettes,
  mechanics, schemas, or governance into accepted Nomos code;
- no general plugin framework or adopter registry;
- no replacement of the adopter's rules, server, persistence, networking,
  protocol, client, or renderer;
- no selection of browser, Godot, or another production platform;
- no public release, outside-player admission, or production migration; and
- no weakening of an acceptance condition because the adopter exposes a gap.

## Upstream admission rule

An adopter finding may enter accepted Nomos work only when all of the following
are true:

1. the observed failure or burden is recorded against exact adopter and Nomos
   inputs;
2. the claim is restated without Mortal Estate identity, content, or assumed
   mechanics;
3. a minimal Nomos-owned fixture reproduces it;
4. the owner classifies it as an existing-contract defect or separately
   authorizes a reusable capability or epoch change;
5. accepted implementation and proof land in Nomos before the adopter updates;
6. a non-author reruns the exact proof; and
7. the adopter consumes a versioned or digest-pinned result, never an unreviewed
   branch or permanent downstream patch.

Game-specific mappings and mechanics do not enter Nomos merely because they were
expensive. A feature requiring adopter names or assumptions is presumptively an
adopter concern.

## Evidence and stop line

The load-bearing experiment criteria are owned by the approved adoption plan.
At minimum they include the adopter's owner-approved target, an authority audit,
actual-play-size visual judgment, a second matching asset made without compiler
or renderer source edits, measured build and iteration cost, applicable proof in
both repositories, and an independent rerun.

Work pauses for owner disposition on any duplicate gameplay authority,
downstream Nomos fork, raw-transform escape, cross-project payload import,
unreviewed reusable feature, required deeper-runtime replacement, rejected
target, red required proof, or inability to create the second matching asset
without renderer or compiler work.

The only permitted final dispositions are:

1. proceed to a clean browser-presentation implementation plan;
2. proceed to a clean Godot consumer of the same admitted Nomos artifacts;
3. authorize a separately decided narrow R2 based on minimal failing evidence;
4. stop.

None is a game-adoption verdict. Actual adoption still requires the remaining
`THESIS.md` section 22 criteria, including Gate 1 as written unless an explicit
contract repair changes it.

## Effect on existing evidence

None. R1 remains accepted and closed on decision 0019's exact candidate. Gate K
remains failed, round two remains terminated incomplete, and every historical
receipt, tag, contract digest, maintenance proof, and non-claim is preserved.

This decision adds prospective authority for evidence collection only. A failed
or stopped adopter experiment does not falsify the recorded R1 result; a
successful presentation experiment does not extend that result to a game.

## Owner disposition

**Authorize the bounded evidence program, not adoption and not R2.** Permit an
exactly pinned Mortal Estate presentation-adoption experiment under the scope,
upstream-admission rule, proof, and stop line above. Keep R1 closed. Require a
new owner decision for every accepted capability family, later runtime epoch,
deeper adopter boundary, or final game-adoption claim.
